🔎 There is / There are ── 「〜がある・〜がいる」

There is ＋ 1つ（1人）
Yes, there is one seat.（席が1つある）
There are ＋ 2つ（2人）以上
There are many signs.（看板がたくさんある）
過去は was / were
There was a little boy.（男の子が1人いた）
There were signs in English, Chinese, Malay, and Tamil.（看板があった）
「〜はありますか？」
is と there を入れかえる。
Is there a seat for my grandpa? ── Yes, there is.

is か are かは、うしろのものの数で決めるよ。× There is many signs. → ○ There are many signs.
